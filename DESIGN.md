# poqi visual identity

## Selected logo

The identity uses a lowercase geometric `poqi` wordmark with rounded bowls, substantial strokes and complementary p/q forms. The name itself carries the identity; do not append a mascot or terminal pictogram.

- Approved raster: `assets/brand/poqi-logo.png`.
- Prefer a light warm background, such as `#F5F2E9`, behind the dark wordmark. Do not place this dark asset directly on a dark background.
- Preserve the image's aspect ratio, letter spacing and surrounding clear space. Do not stretch or redraw individual letters independently.
- Use the complete wordmark for the first GitHub organization avatar and website branding. An alternate tiny favicon or dark-background mark has not been designed or approved.
- This is an ImageGen-generated PNG, not a vector master or a font selection. Typeface choices for the future website remain open.

## Provenance

The logo is AI-generated. Asset usage guidance is in `assets/brand/README.md`.
